Research profile · NUS

Bio

I am a Research Fellow in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the National University of Singapore.

My research focuses on back-end-of-line compatible oxide-semiconductor devices for monolithic 3D integration, with an emphasis on high-density non-volatile memory and compute-in-memory applications.

Current work includes oxide-semiconductor ferroelectric field-effect transistors, 3D ferroelectric vertical NAND, 2T0C DRAM, and physics-based reliability and variability studies using electrical characterization, TCAD, and compact modeling.
